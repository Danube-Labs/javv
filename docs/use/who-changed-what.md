## The Audit log

1. Open the Audit log from the side menu. It lists each change: when, who, what and the target.
2. To see the changes of one person, filter by user.
3. To see one type of change, filter by action, for example Bulk triage. One bulk change is one
   entry in the Audit log.
4. To save the entries as a file, select Export CSV.

## The history of one finding

1. Open Findings, and open a finding.
2. The Activity card shows the changes that a person made on this one finding, with who and when.
   A bulk change does not show on the Activity card today. Find it in the Audit log.

For more help, open the Guide from the side menu of JAVV.
